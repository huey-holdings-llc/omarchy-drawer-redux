# Third-party software

Omarchy Drawer Redux bundles nothing and adds no dependencies. It runs inside
the Omarchy shell and calls `omarchy-plugin-remove`, which ships with Omarchy.

It continues [Plugin Drawer](https://github.com/alyayman921/UNMAINTAINED-Omarchy-drawer)
by Aly Sarhan, also MIT licensed. The original copyright notice is kept in
`LICENSE`, and the original commit history is kept in this repository.
